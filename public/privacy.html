Durham Coast Incident Hub

This page explains how personal information is handled in the current pilot.

Pilot status
Do not use the system for live sensitive or enforcement information until Durham County Council has approved the final lawful basis, retention period and partner data-sharing arrangements.

What is collected

Incident informationType, description, time, location and follow-up.
Reporter informationName and organisation where needed. A member of the public may report without giving a name.
Optional evidencePhotos and relevant vehicle or person details only where necessary.
Audit informationAdministrator access, exports and record-management actions.

Why it is used

For incident management, partner coordination, follow-up, evidence handling and broad trend analysis. Information should only be collected where it is necessary and proportionate.

Who can see it

Detailed incident records are restricted to authorised administrators. Non-admin users see only aggregate counts and a deliberately generalised map.

Retention

The app includes retention-review controls, but no operational retention period is set until Information Governance approves one. Records can be placed on hold where there is a legitimate reason to retain them longer.

Sharing

Multi-agency access must be covered by an approved data-sharing arrangement defining each organisation's role and permitted use.

Your rights and questions

Durham County Council's general data-protection information is available at durham.gov.uk/dataprivacy. Data-protection queries can be sent to DPO@durham.gov.uk. You can also raise concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office.

This pilot notice must be replaced or approved as the service-specific privacy notice before operational rollout.
